## The Antigravity Synthesis (Personalized)

*Why does this matter for YOUR goals?*

As an AI researcher focusing on Multi-Agent Systems and RAG, basic data structures like Queues might seem merely foundational, but they are architecturally critical:
1. **Multi-Agent Message Passing:** In frameworks like you're building for `career-ops` or AIDA, agent-to-agent message buses are often conceptually **Queues**. Understanding underflow/overflow helps design robust asynchronous agents.
2. **Reinforcement Learning:** Your work on AURA (RLHF) likely involves Replay Buffers. Efficient implementation of replay buffers is effectively a **Circular Queue**.
3. **Graph Search (BFS):** Navigating complex legal argument trees (ArguGenCourt) or expanding reasoning graphs in LLMs heavily relies on Queue-based Breadth-First Search algorithms.

## Summary

This note covers the fundamentals of three related data structures: the Simple Queue (FIFO), the Circular Queue (space-efficient wrapping), and the Deque (Double-Ended Queue, which generalizes both stacks and queues). It provides algorithms and time-complexity O(1) proofs for array and linked-list implementations.
